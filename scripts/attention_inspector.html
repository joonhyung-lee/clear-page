Reading this view

Colors use one fixed linear scale across observations for the current layer, head and query. Floor and terrain are drawn beneath objects. Start and goal tokens use rings. Colors encode token weight, not probability density per square meter. Hover lists every token under the pointer.

Body and whole-path decision tokens have no single spatial footprint. Their weights remain in the table.

Extraction and verification

Softmax attention from the original trained checkpoint. All four layers and four heads are available. Default aggregation is their arithmetic mean over object queries. Flow aggregation includes only selected object queries. No spatial smoothing or attention rollout is applied.

Robot and object states come from recorded simulation. Attention is recomputed offline at those observations. This is not an online attention recording and does not establish causal importance or task success. Geometry-token attention is distinct from RGB-pixel saliency and from learned depth or segmentation probes.

Extraction checks: identical encoder predictions and generated paths with attention capture enabled, normalized softmax rows, zero weight on masked causal edges, and agreement with the page’s original object-selection predictions.
